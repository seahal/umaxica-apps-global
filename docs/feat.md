# Feature Notes

## Customer / Identity

- The extent to which `customer` should be included in the `Identity` contract is still under
  consideration.
- At the moment, you can share the idea of ​​`status_id` and `withdrawable` as well as `user` /
  `staff`.
- However, `customer` It is undetermined whether to treat it as an apposed subject of auth or as a
  quasi-subject exclusively for inquiries.
- Until this is fixed, auth linkage of `customer` will be kept to a minimum.
